Who is running this study

SafeSpace is part of an academic research study conducted by [research team names] of [institution], under the supervision of [adviser]. The study was reviewed by [ethics review board, approval number]. The research team is the personal information controller for this study.

Why we are collecting this

We want to understand the kinds of stories and dilemmas adults in the Philippines carry, how heavy they feel, and how these differ by age, gender, relationship status, and occupation. Your information is used only for this research and for academic publications that report combined results.

What we collect

  • Your email address and a hashed password, so you can sign in. Researchers never see your email.
  • Your age range, gender, relationship status, and occupation. Each has a “prefer not to say” option.
  • The entries you write: whether it is a story or a dilemma, what it is about, a title, the text, how heavy it feels, the feelings you choose, and whether you have told anyone.
  • The date and version of the consent you gave.

We do not collect your name, birthdate, address, location, phone number, or device identifiers. Because your entries may describe your health, relationships, or other personal matters, we treat everything you write as sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Who can see what

  • Only you can read your entries.
  • Researchers see only totals and percentages: for example, how many entries are about family, or the age ranges of participants. Any group of fewer than three people is hidden.
  • Researchers cannot see your email, your individual answers, or the text of any entry.

Optional AI replies

If you switch them on, each entry you save is sent to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI model, so it can write a supportive reply. The reply is written by an AI. It is not a counselor, psychologist, or psychiatrist, and it cannot give diagnoses or treatment. Anthropic processes this text under its commercial terms as our service provider. Researchers see only how many replies were written and how many were flagged as a possible crisis, never the replies themselves. You can switch AI replies off at any time in your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until the study ends on [end date], then delete it. Only the combined, anonymous results are kept in the research paper. You can erase everything sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this notice);
  • access and correct your information, which you can do on your account page;
  • withdraw consent and have your data erased, using “Erase my account” on your account page;
  • object to processing, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Taking part is voluntary. Withdrawing will not affect you in any way. Data already included in published, combined results cannot be removed from them, but it cannot be traced back to you.

How we protect it

In this simple version, your account and entries are saved only in this web browser on this device. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and the researcher view is limited by design to combined statistics.

Risks and support

Writing about difficult experiences can bring up strong feelings. You can stop at any time. If you are in distress, the NCMH Crisis Hotline is available 24/7 at 1553 or 0917 899 8727.
