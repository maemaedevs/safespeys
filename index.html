What taking part involves

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Just an email and a password. You confirm you are 18 or older.

  2. 2

    Answer four questions and give consent

    Your age range, gender, relationship status, and occupation, each with a “prefer not to say” option. You read the privacy notice and agree to take part.

  3. 3

    Write, whenever you need to

    Share a story about something that happened or a dilemma you are facing. Come back to add more, edit, or delete.

You can switch on an optional, clearly labelled AI reply to each entry. You can stop at any time and erase your account. Everything you wrote is deleted with it.
